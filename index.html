Hubitat Nest

Hubitat Nest is a personal app that connects my own Nest thermostat to my Hubitat home hub. It is used only by me. No data is collected, stored or shared with anyone.

Privacy policy

This app is for my personal use only. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Thermostat information stays between my Google account and my own home hub.

Terms of service

This app is not offered to the public and is provided as is for my own personal use.
